The engine

Three layers. No black box magic.

Every recommendation has a practical explanation so a person can understand why a donor appears near the top.

01

Screen

Blood-group compatibility, conservative demo age rules, donation recency and distance are checked before prediction.

02

Prioritize

The ML model predicts response likelihood. Availability and proximity help turn that prediction into an operational shortlist.

03

Learn

Accepted, declined and completed outcomes can be stored as historical interaction data for future model evaluation.
