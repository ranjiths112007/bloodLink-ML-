The engine

Three layers. No black-box medical decision.

The model is only a prioritization signal. Hard screening stays separate and the final eligibility decision stays with an authorised professional.

01

Screen

Check blood-group compatibility, conservative demo eligibility rules and distance before a candidate reaches the model.
